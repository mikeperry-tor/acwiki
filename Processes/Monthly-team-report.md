# Monthly team report

At the beginning of each month, the anti-censorship team creates a monthly report that summarises the accomplishments of the previous month.  We publish these reports on the tor-project mailing list and on the blog. Here are a few examples:
* July 2020 [blog](https://blog.torproject.org/anti-censorship-july-2020) & [mailing list](https://lists.torproject.org/pipermail/tor-project/2020-August/002955.html)
* June 2020 [blog](https://blog.torproject.org/anti-censorship-june-2020) & [mailing list](https://lists.torproject.org/pipermail/tor-project/2020-July/002928.html)

## Process

1. At the beginning of the month, create a [Riseup pad](https://pad.riseup.net) and ask the anti-censorship team to add their work highlights to the pad. It's best to do that as an announcement on the [IRC meeting pad](https://pad.riseup.net/p/tor-anti-censorship-keep).
2. Once you have all work highlights, turn them into a coherent report. It's best to use the markdown format because it can easily be turned into HTML, which you can then copy & paste into the blog.
3. Ask the anti-censorship team to review the report before posting it.
4. After the review, send the report to the tor-project mailing list and write a blog post. Note that for our monthly report we don't need a review from the communications team.
5. Consider asking the communications team to tweet a link to our monthly report. Simply send an email to comms@tp.o.
